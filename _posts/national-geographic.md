Wow — what a year!

Last month I finished my internship at National Geographic UK TV. It felt pretty surreal to
have been an intern somewhere so close to my heart, somewhere so iconic, and somewhere
genuinely investing in passionate people & stories.

A HUGE thank you to the fantastic Nat Geo team who mentored me. I really did feel pushed to
reach my potential. I learnt how to support 8 creative producers, communicate with studios
across time zones, book talent, deliver to TX deadlines, and get to grips with budgets. Plus,
throughout the year, I was encouraged to maintain my curiosity beyond the role — from
development and commissioning, to the National Geographic Society.

The community, belief, and encouragement I received made this internship so special. I've left
feeling like I truly made the most of my 12 months there, and it's an experience I'll carry with
me (alongside enough branded merch to last my whole career).
